Bela Krajina dialects are Slovene dialects of south-east Slovenia.[^1]

## Overview {#overview}

The dialects are spoken in Bela Krajina, a region bordering Croatia.[^1]
They are usually grouped with the Lower Carniolan dialects.[^2]

## Metlika dialect {#metlika}

The Metlika dialect is centred on the town of Metlika.[^2]

## Črnomelj dialect {#crnomelj}

The Črnomelj dialect is centred on the town of Črnomelj.[^2]

## Standard language {#standard}

Standard written Slovene grew with print; see [[printing-press#effects|effects of printing]].

## Key claims {#key-claims}

- Bela Krajina dialects are Slovene dialects of south-east Slovenia.[^1]
- They include the Metlika and Črnomelj dialects.[^2]
