Photosynthesis converts light energy into chemical energy stored in sugar.[^1]

## Inputs and outputs {#inputs-outputs}

Plants take in carbon dioxide and water.[^1]
They release oxygen as a by-product.[^1]
The overall equation is 6 CO2 + 6 H2O + light → C6H12O6 + 6 O2.[^2]

## Where it happens {#chloroplasts}

In plants, photosynthesis takes place in chloroplasts.[^2]
Chloroplasts contain the green pigment chlorophyll.[^2]

## Why it matters {#why-it-matters}

Photosynthesis produces the oxygen in the atmosphere.[^3]
Printed botany texts spread this knowledge after the [[printing-press|printing press]].

## Key claims {#key-claims}

- Photosynthesis releases oxygen.[^1]
- It happens in chloroplasts in plants.[^2]
- Photosynthesis produces the oxygen in the atmosphere.[^3]
