## Answer {#answer}

Human anatomy is the scientific study of the structures of the body.[^1]
Anatomists describe the body in six levels, from chemicals to the whole organism.[^2]
They always describe the body in one standard posture, the anatomical position.[^3]
They use three main planes and named body cavities to give locations.[^6]

## Key insights {#key-insights}

- Anatomy studies structures, and physiology studies how these structures work.[^1]
- The body has four main tissue types: epithelial, connective, muscle and nervous.[^5]
- Sources do not agree on the number of organ systems: 10 or 11.[^2][^4]
- Directional terms always refer to the anatomical position.[^3]
- The adult skeleton usually has 206 named bones.[^7]

## Anatomy and physiology {#what-anatomy-is}

Anatomy studies the structures of the body.[^1]
Physiology studies how these structures work and support the functions of life.[^1]
Some structures are very small, so you can study them only with a microscope.[^1]

## Levels of organisation {#levels}

Textbooks often describe the body in six levels.[^2]
Each level is more complex than the level before it:[^2]

1. chemical
2. cellular
3. tissue
4. organ
5. organ system
6. organism

An organ is a structure made of two or more types of tissue.[^2]
An organ system is a group of organs that work together.[^2]
The organism, a whole living person, is the highest level.[^2]
The diagram shows how each level builds on the level before it.[^2]

## Tissues {#tissues}

A tissue is a group of similar cells that work together as a unit.[^5]
The body has four main tissue types:[^5]

- epithelial tissue
- connective tissue
- muscle tissue
- nervous tissue

For example, the wall and lining of the stomach contain all four types.[^4]

## Organ systems {#organ-systems}

Textbooks do not all count organ systems in the same way.[^2][^4]
The training course of the U.S. National Cancer Institute lists 10 major systems:[^4]

- skeletal
- muscular
- nervous
- endocrine
- cardiovascular
- lymphatic
- respiratory
- digestive
- urinary
- reproductive

The OpenStax textbook counts 11 organ systems.[^2]
One of its additions is the integumentary system: the skin, hair and nails.[^2]

## Anatomical position {#anatomical-position}

The anatomical position is the standard reference posture of the body.[^3]
The body stands upright, with the feet parallel and the toes pointing forward.[^3]
The arms are held out to each side, and the palms face forward.[^3]
Directional terms such as "anterior" (front) always refer to this position.[^3]

## Body planes {#planes}

Anatomists use planes to divide the body or any of its parts.[^6]
There are three main planes:[^6]

- **Sagittal plane:** vertical, runs from front to back. It divides the body into right and left parts.
- **Coronal (frontal) plane:** vertical, runs from side to side. It divides the body into front (anterior) and back (posterior) parts.
- **Transverse (axial) plane:** horizontal. It divides the body into upper and lower parts.

The median plane is the sagittal plane through the midline.[^6]
It divides the body into two equal halves.[^6]

## Body cavities {#cavities}

Body cavities are spaces in the body that contain the internal organs.[^6]
The two main body cavities are the ventral cavity and the dorsal cavity.[^6]
The ventral cavity is the larger of the two.[^6]
The diaphragm, a dome-shaped muscle for breathing, divides it into two parts:[^6]

- The **thoracic cavity** holds the heart, the lungs, the trachea and the oesophagus.
- The **abdominopelvic cavity** has an abdominal part and a pelvic part. The abdominal part holds most of the digestive tract and the kidneys. The pelvic part holds most of the urinary and reproductive organs and the rectum.

The dorsal cavity also has two parts:[^6]

- The **cranial cavity** holds the brain.
- The **vertebral canal** holds the spinal cord.

## Skeleton {#skeleton}

The adult skeleton usually has 206 named bones.[^7]
The skeleton has two divisions:[^7]

- **Axial skeleton, 80 bones:** the bones of the head, the vertebral column, the ribs and the sternum.
- **Appendicular skeleton, 126 bones:** the limbs and the girdles that attach them to the axial skeleton.

## Key claims {#key-claims}

- Anatomy studies the structures of the body, and physiology studies how they work.[^1]
- The body is described in six levels, from chemical to organism.[^2]
- There are four main tissue types: epithelial, connective, muscle and nervous.[^5]
- Sources differ on the number of organ systems: 10 in the NCI course, 11 in OpenStax.[^2][^4]
- In the anatomical position, the body stands upright with the palms facing forward.[^3]
- The adult skeleton usually has 206 bones: 80 axial and 126 appendicular.[^7]
