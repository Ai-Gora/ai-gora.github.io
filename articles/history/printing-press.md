Johannes Gutenberg developed a movable-type printing press around 1440.[^1][^2]
By 1450, his press in Mainz was working.[^2]

## Invention {#invention}

Gutenberg combined these parts:[^1]

- metal movable type
- oil-based ink
- a screw press

He completed his Bible in 1455.[^2]

## Effects {#effects}

Printing lowered the cost of books.[^1]
It helped standardise written languages.[^1]
For the first printed books in Slovene, see [[bela-krajina-dialects#standard|Bela Krajina dialects]].
Scientific works such as texts on [[photosynthesis]] later reached wide audiences in print.

## Key claims {#key-claims}

- Gutenberg's press dates to around 1440.[^2]
- Printing lowered the cost of books.[^1]
