Johannes Gutenberg developed a movable-type printing press in Mainz around 1440.[^2]

## Invention {#invention}

Gutenberg combined metal movable type, oil-based ink and a screw press.[^1]
His Bible was printed around 1455.[^2]

## Effects {#effects}

Printing lowered the cost of books.[^1]
It helped standardise written languages, including Slovene; see [[bela-krajina-dialects#overview|Bela Krajina dialects]].[^1]
Scientific works such as texts on [[photosynthesis]] later reached wide audiences in print.

## Key claims {#key-claims}

- Gutenberg's press dates to around 1440.[^2]
- Printing lowered the cost of books.[^1]
